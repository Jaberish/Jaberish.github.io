Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 7, 2026

IN SHORT

  • We don't collect your personal information. There are no accounts, and we run no servers.
  • Your progress (snacks, trophies, settings) is saved on your phone only.
  • Party mode connects phones on the same Wi-Fi to each other. Nothing goes beyond that network.
  • The app shows ads from Google AdMob. Google may use information from your device to show and measure those ads, as explained below. On iPhone and iPad, the ads aren't personalized and the app never asks to track you.

This policy explains how the game Midnight Snack Attack, for Android and for iPhone and iPad (com.hutstacks.midnightsnackattack on both), made by HutStacks ("we", "us"), handles information. By using the app, you agree to this policy.

INFORMATION WE COLLECT

We don't collect, store, or receive any personal information about you. The app has no sign-in, no accounts, and no servers of ours that it talks to.

WHAT STAYS ON YOUR PHONE

So the game remembers you, it saves the following on your device, and only there:

  • your progress: tournaments played and won, crumbs, unlocked snacks, trophies, and how well you do at each minigame;
  • how hard the computer players are, which adjusts as you play;
  • your settings: language, sound, music, vibration, and eco mode;
  • when you last saw an ad, so ads aren't shown too often;
  • when the game last asked you to rate it, so it doesn't ask too often.

This information is never sent to us. You can delete it at any time by deleting the app, or on Android by clearing the app's data in its settings.

PARTY MODE (PLAYING WITH FRIENDS ON THE SAME WI-FI)

When you host a party, your phone runs a small connection point that only phones on the same Wi-Fi network can reach. It announces the party on that network under a name like "Waffles' party" so nearby phones can list it. Joining phones work out the host's local network address from a party code made of snack pictures.

During a party, the phones exchange only what the game needs: which snacks you picked, your button presses, and the game's results. This traffic stays on your local network, isn't sent to us or anyone else, and isn't kept after the party ends. Android and iPhone players can join the same party. To do this, the app uses Android's network permissions (internet access, Wi-Fi state, and Wi-Fi multicast for finding parties). On iPhone and iPad, it asks your permission to find and connect to devices on your local network the first time you open a party. You can change that later in Settings > Privacy & Security > Local Network.

ADVERTISING

The app is free and shows a full-screen ad now and then, between games and never during play. Ads are provided by Google AdMob, a service of Google LLC. To show ads, measure how they perform, limit how often you see them, and prevent fraud, Google may collect or receive information from your device, such as:

  • on Android, your device's advertising ID (on iPhone and iPad, the app never asks to track you, so Google doesn't get your device's advertising identifier, and the ads aren't personalized);
  • your IP address, and the approximate location it suggests;
  • information about your device and the app, such as the model, operating system, language, and app version;
  • how you interact with the ads, such as whether you tapped one.

Google handles this information under its own privacy policy. To learn more, see how Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites and the Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

YOUR CHOICES ABOUT ADS

  • Consent: where the law requires it (for example in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, and Switzerland), the app asks for your consent before personalized ads are shown, using Google's consent tool. You can change your choice at any time with the "Privacy choices" button on the app's Trophy Shelf.
  • Your advertising ID: on Android, you can delete or reset your advertising ID, or opt out of personalized ads, under Settings > Privacy > Ads (the exact path varies by device).
  • Tracking: on iPhone and iPad, the app never asks for permission to track you, and only asks for ads that aren't personalized.

If you don't consent to personalized ads, you may still see ads that aren't based on your interests.

RATINGS AND REVIEWS

Now and then, after you win a tournament, the app may show the rating card of Google Play (on Android) or the App Store (on iPhone and iPad). Any rating or review you give goes straight to Google or Apple under your store account, and they handle it under their own privacy policies. The app never sees it; like everyone else, we only see the reviews the store shows.

CHILDREN

Midnight Snack Attack is a game for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. We don't knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, contact us and we'll help.

SECURITY AND RETENTION

Because we don't collect your information, we don't store or keep any of it. What's saved on your phone stays there until you clear the app's data or delete the app. Information Google receives through ads is kept according to Google's policies.

CHANGES TO THIS POLICY

If we change how the app handles information, we'll update this policy and change the date at the top. Significant changes will also be noted in the app's store listings.
