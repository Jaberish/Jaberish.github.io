PARTY HELP

  • Every phone or tablet has to be on the same Wi-Fi.
  • The host taps PARTY, then HOST A PARTY. Friends tap PARTY and pick the party from their list.
  • Party not in the list? Tap ENTER A PARTY CODE and tap the snacks shown on the host's screen, in order.
  • On iPhone and iPad, allow Local Network when the game asks. If you said no, turn it on in Settings > Apps > Midnight Snack Attack > Local Network.
  • Guest, hotel and school Wi-Fi often stop devices from seeing each other. A home Wi-Fi works best.
  • Everyone needs the same version of the game. If a phone says the versions differ, update the game on every phone.

YOUR PROGRESS

Snacks, trophies and settings are saved on your device only. Deleting the game deletes them too.

When you email us, please say which phone or tablet you're using, and what happened. A screenshot helps.
